Inktober 2026

Blog

Hi! I’m Dee Kay, a shy young artist dreaming of earning money from my art. I like to paint and illustrate.

For this inktober I decided to strictly follow rules and used drawing ink. I remember how I wasn’t used to its drying time and that you cannot do gradient effects like with watercolors.



Day 1 Apple

For this prompt I sketched ideas first using references from pinterest (You can see in the sketch page below), my idea was to portray a vampire queen or something like that. I had to practise drawing an open mouth, which I don’t draw often. I remember having a mistake in lineart that I tried to fix, if you can see that.



Day 2 Relic

As a relic I chose to portray an old camera, I remember being given one for a school trip back in the days when phones didn’t have built in cameras.



Day 3 Miniature

I had a couple ideas for this illustration. I was inspired by the movie “Up” the scene where a little girl saws a house flying because of the balloons. I remember that I was still trying to learn how to draw using ink’s values.



Day 4 Cactus and day 5 Smack

I decided to combine illustrations on one page. For cactus I decided to involve a space theme similar to what I did with last year's Inktober's onion illustration. For smack I tried to make it simple and made a comic inspired drawing, lettering the word SMACK using a small brush.



Day 6 Ogre

I had sketched the sketch of this illustration before realizing that I can combine it with the “bake” prompt. In the end, here's a nice story of how ogre helps little girls bake cupcakes.



Day 7 Panic

So for this illustration I made an exception and used black water color instead of ink to create the splashes on the page. It's a portrayal of this kinda quiet panic where from the outside the person looks calm, but from inside there’s loud anxiety as storming ocean waves.



Day 8 Stinky

For this day's prompt I decided to illustrate a person waiting for a bath to fill up with water so she could use a bath. And yes she has no mouth. For this I remember messing up lineart around her arms so I used white acrylic paint to cover it up ( I actually heard this advice from youtube.)



Day 9 Ram

I decided to illustrate an anthropomorphic ram. I actually looked up pictures of Soothsayer from Kung Fu Panda 2 and Gazelle from Zootopia ( who’s not a ram ) but I needed a reference of hands.. I used different brand of fineliners for this illustration and I wasn’t sure they wont bleed in contact with water.



Day 10 Mystical

Here's the illustration of a young witch holding stuff with mystical crystal in it. For this illustration I feel that I really pushed contrast by having very dark areas where I applied a very thick layer of ink. I also started to understand why comics made with ink have this posterized effect (when light, midshadows and shadow tones are divided with a clear edge), the ink just works that way.



Day 11 Rescue

What came to my mind about this prompt is prince saving a princess scene.. but i decided to make it reverse by making an illustration of princess with robo suit saving a prince.



Day 12 Toss

For this day I wanted to paint something that resembled watercolour landscape painting ( using ink of course). So here’s an illustration of a girl throwing rocks in a river surrounded by mountains and clouds.



Day 13 Flimsy

I don’t fully remember it but I remember a poem in my native language about the moon building a bridge and a line from a poem saying “ I’m sorry moon, but I won’t go through your bridge” and it kinda inspired me for the illustration of a woman going through a flimsy bridge while moon watches her.



Day 14 Lady

I think that a flapper or 1920s fashion woman associates me with the word “lady”. At first I wanted to illustrate Miss Mitzi from Lackadaisy as a human because she really suits the word “lady”, despite the fact that she’s a cat. ( you can see the sketch for Mitzi below). I stick with the idea to illustrate an art deco inspired woman. I decided to use salt on her scarf and dress for making a texture. This far in Inktober I wanted to experiment with texture because making art only in black started to become boring.



Day 15 Hooray

I remember I wasn’t in the mood for this type of illustration. I feel that the sketch for this illustration was more expressive of this joy feeling. This is a person getting a package from an e-shop and showing a little smile for it. I think that I was tired of drawing eyes at this point so we’re good without them.



Day 16 Gangly

As an illustrator, I try to make a story within illustration. This one’s story is about a little girl finding a toy cat in a box and being surprised how gangly it is. I created this distorted frame to emphasize how long this toy cat is. I deliberately colored the toy cat darker, while the rest of the illustration is lighter to emphasize the main element of the illustration - the gangly toy cat.



Day 17 Contraption



Here’s a fun thing. I'm not native english speaker so I had a picture of a prompt list translated by google lens and for this prompt it was translated as “contract” so I drew this illustration of a witch holding a contract desperately wanting you to sign it. It was while watching other Inktober participants on youtube that I rechecked and realized what the word “contraption” really meant. The moral of the story is it is better to doublecheck, search the definition of the word as well.

So I made another illustration for this theme ( even though contraptions are meant to be small sized so I'm not sure if this is correct either). I created a couple sketches for this one, but I based the illustration on my wild story idea of a super computer that would control pocket dimensions or something like that.



Day 18 Flightless

I had a lot of ideas for this one but I decided to stick to this millimanistic one. The idea was to portray a person thinking of flying in a plane. In sketches you can see the idea of drawing a plane inside a thought bubble but I scrapped that. I haven’t drawn planes before and that’s the thing I like about Inktober because you have a chance to learn to draw something new that you haven’t drawn before.



Day 19 Confused

It’s a confused astronaut in space. I remember trying to use salt crystals for this too, but they stuck to a paper and when I rubbed them off they damaged the paper and I used white acrylic paint to kinda fix the paper and paint something resembling a milky way. I based this illustration on how I like to describe this feeling of being lost and confused as being this astronaut in space where you don’t know where you are waiting for signals from your ship wondering when will they pull you back in the ship as waiting for clarity.. I also wanted to add a lyric of the song Astronaut by Simple Plan but decided not to to keep illustration more universal.



Day 20 Lounge

I remember once being in a lounge in school- it had a similar couch as in illustration. would be a nice quiet place to read huh.



Day 21 Hero

This prompt was a headache.. I remembered a Shrek 2 movie, an iconic godmother's performance singing a song “Holding Out For a Hero”. So I decided to illustrate a singer singing the lyrics for a song.



Day 22 Beacon

For this illustration I used a reference photo of a local beacon and added those stormy waves.



Day 23 Dapper

I didn’t know what the word “dapper” meant until now. My idea was to portray a dapper man drawing in his sketchbook. I don’t know why but this turned out a little bit creepy.





Day 25 Fracture

So for this piece I was inspired by Frida Kahlo, I took inspiration from her face features. Another inspiration was my dream of breaking a rib bone. I added butterflies in this illustration for fun, but they also could be a symbol of change or renewal. Mixing shades of black with ink is still confusing to me though.



Day 26 Zip

Do you remember when a mom zips up your jacket when you were a kid, I remember that once she zipped it the way that zipper bit me under the chin, so I learned to tilt up my head when I zip a jacked.. So anyway, here's an illustration of a kid who gets his jacked zipped by his mom. I remember using black pencil to add finishing touches.



Day 27 Dumb

I wanted to make a kinda funny comic for this prompt. So it’s about this person who is driving listening to phone’s map instructions and he gets to a mountain right in the middle of the road so he gets out of the car and starts to argue with a mountain about that according to a phone it should not be there or maybe argues to the phone that there’s a mountain and that it's stupid. At first I wanted to illustrate the person with a map but I thought that phone would be more relevant nowadays.



Day 28 Trophy

By one of the definitions of the word "trophy" is- something that was obtained from the enemy during wartime.. So this is an illustration of a warrior taking a sword from an armor laying on the ground.. For these illustrations I tried to make some sorta interesting frame in them, but here I felt that illustration needed to “breath” and I didn't put a frame for this one.



Day 29 Tusk

I had a couple of sketches for this one’s composition ( as you can see in the sketch below) . I drew one of my OC’s, her name is Lesley. As a tusk I thought that it could be the teeth of those demons that samurai masks have.



Day 30 Cookie

For this I decided to draw a cute little fairy eating gigantic cookie. In an earlier sketch I sketched an older fairy, but decided that a kid fairy would make more sense. If you look at it for long enough you can hear the cookie crunch. Wouldn't it be a cute sticker?



Day 31 Flex

This word has a variety of meanings. I chose to portray “Flex” as a short form of a word Flexible. I illustrated a girl doing Tai Chi. I've been trying to learn Tai Chi and I saw this particular Tai Chi exercise on the internet and thought that for this one you have to be pretty flexible to be able to do that.
